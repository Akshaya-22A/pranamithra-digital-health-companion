# Prana Mithra
“Bridging Tradition and Technology for Community Health”

### **Purpose**
Prana Mithra is a **culturally rooted, AI-powered health application** designed for rural and tribal communities. It empowers **ASHA workers**, families, and individuals by combining **modern healthcare tools with traditional knowledge**, ensuring accessible, proactive, and reliable health support in local languages.

### **What is the Idea?**
Prana Mithra acts as a **digital health companion** that helps people check symptoms, scan visuals, learn about herbal medicine, access maternal & child health guides, and find nearby hospitals or pharmacies in real time. It integrates **AI, GPS, multilingual support, and cultural storytelling (“Katha”)** to provide health education and guidance in a familiar, relatable format. It works in **3 languages (English, Telugu, Odia)** and **2 tribal dialects (Koya, Konda)**

### **Key Features**

* 🌐 **Multilingual Support** → Works in **4 languages (English, Hindi, Telugu, Odia)** and **2 tribal dialects (Koya, Konda)**.
* 🎙️ **Voice Input & Text-to-Speech** → Users can **speak** to the app or listen to guidance read aloud.
* 🤖 **AI Symptom Checker** → Instant preliminary health advice by describing symptoms (text or voice).
* 📸 **Visual Symptom Scanner** → Upload images (wounds, rashes) for AI-based analysis.
* 🌿 **Herbal Identifier** → Identifies local medicinal plants & explains traditional uses.
* 🤰 **Maternal & Child Health Timeline** → Pregnancy milestones + child vaccination reminders.
* 🚨 **Pregnancy Risk Alerts** → Helps ASHA workers detect high-risk pregnancies early.
* 🦠 **Outbreak Prediction** → Predicts possible community disease outbreaks from local reports.
* 📖 **Health “Katha” Stories** → Interactive, folk-style storytelling with local dialect voiceovers & animations for education on hygiene, vaccination, maternal care.
* 🏥 **Facility Locator** → Find nearby hospitals, clinics, and doctors with GPS & contact info.
* 💊 **Pharmacy Tracker** → Shows availability of essential medicines in local pharmacies.
* 🏢 **ASHA Dashboard** → Task management, alerts, and patient monitoring for frontline workers.
* 📑 **Health Schemes** → Easy guides on government healthcare schemes & eligibility.
* 📡 **Real-Time Location & Bed Availability** → Tracks nearest healthcare facility with available beds.

### **Impact**

* 🧑‍⚕️ **Empowers ASHA workers** with AI-driven tools to save time and improve decision-making.
* 🌍 **Accessible to low-literacy users** through voice, dialect support, and cultural storytelling.
* 🚑 **Improves healthcare access** by providing real-time facility and medicine information.
* 🌱 **Preserves traditional knowledge** by integrating local herbal remedies and stories.
* 🛡️ **Proactive care** with outbreak prediction and early pregnancy risk detection.
